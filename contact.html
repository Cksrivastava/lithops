FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick Answers to Your Inquiries

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for contract manufacturing?
Our MOQs vary depending on the dosage form. Typically, for tablets & capsules, it starts from 50,000 to 1,00,000 units, and for liquids/syrups around 2,000 to 5,000 bottles. We are flexible to support new growing brands.
How long does the production and delivery cycle take?
For repeat orders, turnaround is usually 20 to 30 days. For new product development requiring artwork approval and packaging fabrication, it takes approximately 35 to 45 days.
Do you provide regulatory dossiers for foreign export registrations?
Yes, our dedicated regulatory affairs team provides complete CTD and ACTD dossiers, COPP, Free Sale Certificates, and batch stability analysis reports for global health authorities.
What documents are required to start third-party manufacturing?
You will need a valid Drug License (Form 20B/21B), GST Registration Certificate, Company PAN Card, and Trademark Registration or Authorization letter for your brand name.
