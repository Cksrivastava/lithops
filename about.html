WHO WE ARE

Pioneering Advanced Healthcare & Pharmaceutical Manufacturing

Established with a clear vision to redefine pharmaceutical contract manufacturing, Associated Biotech has evolved into one of the country's most respected third-party manufacturing partners. We cater to domestic pharmaceutical giants as well as international healthcare distributors.

Our cutting-edge manufacturing facility adheres to strict WHO-GMP and ISO guidelines, operating sophisticated fully automated lines capable of producing tablets, capsules, dry syrups, liquid injectables, softgels, ointments, and herbal formulations.
